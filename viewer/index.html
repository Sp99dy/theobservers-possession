What these numbers mean

spellF1 (ranking metric): possessions as objects, matched by time IoU, F1 averaged over IoU 0.3–0.7. macroF1: frame-exact per-class F1 for H/A/D. Possessions: number of H/A runs, and their mean length. The public window is only 5 minutes (≈17 possessions), so one possession more or less moves spellF1 a lot; we never tune on it.
